About ScholarMatch

An intelligent, student-oriented platform designed to make finding education welfare schemes seamless and transparent.

⚠️

The Problem

Millions of deserving students miss out on scholarships worth crores of rupees every year because eligibility rules are fragmented across dozens of disconnected departmental websites and complex notifications.

💡

Our Solution

ScholarMatch acts as a single-window algorithmic matching engine that analyzes academic, income, social category, and domicile criteria to instantly calculate qualifying schemes with scored transparency.

🎁

The Impact

Students save dozens of hours, avoid filing disqualified applications, and receive step-by-step guidance on required documentation and official portal links.

  • Score 50% – 74%: Potential Match (🟡) — Near threshold with conditional relaxation or warnings shown.
  • Score < 50% or Hard Disqualification: Not Eligible (🔴) — Specific disqualifying reasons displayed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an RD Number on certificates?

An RD (Revenue Department) number is an electronic 15-character barcode reference issued by the Revenue Department (e.g., Bhoomi or Nadakacheri in Karnataka, MahaDBT in Maharashtra) that allows online automated verification without physical paperwork.

Why is Aadhaar Bank Seeding (DBT) mandatory?

All central and state government scholarships disburse funds via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the NPCI Aadhaar payment bridge to ensure direct credit into the student's active bank account.

Can I apply for more than one scholarship?

As per Ministry regulations, a student can generally avail of only one government tuition fee scholarship at a time, though certain merit incentives or private CSR grants may be combined depending on scheme bylaws.

Is ScholarMatch an official government portal?

No, ScholarMatch is an independent academic informational decision support tool. It checks your criteria and guides you to verified official portals (NSP, SSP, AICTE) for actual submissions.
